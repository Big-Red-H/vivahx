HIM 0.2.0 looks and sounds more like AIM, and adds dark mode, your own colors, and drag-and-drop file sending.

**New**
- **The AIM IM window:** your buddy's icon sits in the lower-left corner with no frame, on the light gray it was drawn for.
- **The classic AIM sounds** are built in: buddy in and out, IM sent and received. Setup still takes your own sound files.
- **Dark mode and your own colors:** Setup > Theme picks Classic or Dark, and **Colors...** lets you change any color in HIM, from text and windows to links and the gray behind Buddy Icons.
- **Drag and drop:** drop a picture or file on an IM window to send it.
- **Choose where received files go:** Setup > Received files (Downloads/HIM unless you change it).
- **Links:** web addresses in IMs and chat rooms are clickable and open in your browser.
- **Chat rooms:** lines relayed from Discord on MacDomain, and from the Hotline Central Hub's Relay, show the real sender: "Tagban: lol (via Discord)".
- **Update notice:** HIM checks GitHub at startup and shows a one-line notice when a newer version is out. It sends nothing about you.
- **BugBot:** a suggested buddy for bug reports and ideas. Tell it what happened, send a screenshot if you have one, and it files the report on GitHub.

**Fixed**
- Linux: clicking a window's title bar (easy to do when a buddy request pops up under the mouse) could leave the window stuck to the pointer.
- Linux: the Flatpak now includes the BadassBuddy icon collection.
