Hello again!

Alright, b30! Grab a coffee, this is a long list. Since b29, Hotline has picked up private chats, Markdown nearly everywhere you write, a much more capable Files section, streaming audio and video previews, and a pile of fixes and small touches.

As promised last time, Hotline now requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later. Keeping to the two most recent macOS releases lets me use Apple's latest and keeps the project fresh and easier to maintain into the future.

### New in b30:
- Private chats! Start a private chat with one or more people on a server, add a subject if you like, and invite others to the conversation later if needed. Private chats get their own spot in the sidebar.
- Markdown everywhere: the message board, news, private messages, broadcasts, and your auto-reply can all now be written in Markdown, styled as you type, with a formatting bar for bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, code, and links. Chat styles Markdown as you type too, and there's a new Format menu in the menu bar. Pasting rich text turns it into Markdown.
- Redesigned message board composer. Quotes, lists, and highlighted code blocks now show up on the board, and selecting text in posts works much better. Drop a picture onto a post to turn it into ASCII art (experimental if not a little silly).
