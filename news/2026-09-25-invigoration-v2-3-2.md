## StarCraft II clans and groups

- **Clans & Groups**, next to **+**: every clan and group your character is in, with your rank, the member count and its description. Press **+** on one to list its members by name and rank; members who come online, join a game or change rank update as it happens.
- **Clan chat** opens as a channel tab, as it does in the game; **Open Chat** does the same for a group.
- Names show as StarCraft II shows them: `<TAG> Name`, with the clan tag a little smaller and no `#123` code. In the Full user list, each person's BattleTag sits underneath.

## StarCraft: Remastered

- **Stats**: `/stats Name`, or right-click someone and choose **Stats**. You get wins and losses (overall, ranked and classic), games played, play time, APM and when the character was made.
- **Check if Online** (right-click): someone listed in the channel who isn't really logged on (a login stuck by a server reset) is hidden from the user list. Typing `/whois` does the same.
- The user list's right-click menu works again: right-clicking a person selects them first.

## Chat

- **Message length limits**, with a counter in the send box: 255 characters for StarCraft II, 223 for classic Battle.net and StarCraft: Remastered. Longer text is cut at the limit.
- StarCraft II doesn't send unknown `/commands` as chat any more. Start a line with `//` to send one that begins with `/`.
- A new channel tab starts with "Entered *channel*.", instead of a blank log.
