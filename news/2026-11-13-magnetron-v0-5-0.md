## What's Changed
Magnetron now supports TLS as well as non-TLS (classic) servers & clients. Using an unused set of bytes, modern servers and clients can support TLS. Magnetron will now let clients know which servers it is tracking support TLS. 

Thanks to both @jhalter and @mierau for their help with this feature. 

* Feature/21 reserved bytes by @benabernathy in https://github.com/benabernathy/magnetron/pull/23


**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/benabernathy/magnetron/compare/v0.4.0...v0.5.0
